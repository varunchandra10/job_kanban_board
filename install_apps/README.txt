================================================================================
               JOB TRACKER - WINDOWS DESKTOP EDITION
================================================================================

Welcome to Job Tracker for Windows! This package provides everything you need to
track your job applications, tailored resumes, and daily interview prep offline
or seamlessly synced with the cloud.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📦 PACKAGE CONTENTS & CHOICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Job-Tracker-Setup.exe  (⭐ RECOMMENDED)
   - 1-Click Windows Installer.
   - Installs Job Tracker to your system, creates Desktop & Start Menu shortcuts.
   - Ideal for permanent, daily job search management.

2. JobTracker.exe         (PORTABLE EDITION)
   - Standalone portable application.
   - No installation required! Double-click and run immediately from any folder
     or external USB drive.

3. Uninstall-Job-Tracker.bat
   - Clean 1-click uninstaller script.
   - Removes shortcuts, cleans registry associations, and cleans up old versions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔒 PRIVACY & CUSTOM LOCAL STORAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- 100% Private & Offline by default: Powered by an embedded local SQLite database.
- User-Defined Documents Folder: You can store your uploaded resumes and cover
  letters in ANY directory or secondary drive (e.g., D:\JobDocuments or E:\).
  This keeps your sensitive files safe and separated from your Windows C: drive.
- You can change or browse your storage location anytime directly from the
  Resume & Documents section inside any application card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔄 1-CLICK GOOGLE DRIVE MIRRORING & TWO-WAY SYNC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Need cloud backup or multi-device access?
- Switch between Local Storage and your personal Google Drive with 1 click.
- Use the built-in "Mirror & Sync" feature to copy all local documents to Google
  Drive, or download Google Drive documents to your local PC hard drive anytime.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚡ WINDOWS SMARTSCREEN NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If Windows displays "Windows protected your PC":
1. Click "More info".
2. Click "Run anyway".
(Standard for newly built independent software without an enterprise certificate).
